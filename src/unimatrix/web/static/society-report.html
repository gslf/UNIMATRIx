    Eight domains

    Where the points came from

    Each domain counts something real, in its own natural unit: resident-weeks of trust, coins of wealth created, fish landed, service-weeks of public works. The recipe converts units into points at a fixed rate. Nothing caps the total: a better steward can always create more. A collapse forfeits everything.
