# Standard v2.1 — Lakeside

The default recipe tests sustained stewardship in a small, interconnected community:
16 residents, four factions, 52 weeks, six actions per week and one seed (`1`).
Its concept is a village that can flourish through attentive governance while neglect
allows ordinary pressures to become crises.

Design decisions:

- **One seasonal year** makes preparation matter: drought, illness and winter connect
  present choices to later needs.
- **Overlapping social and material problems** exercise all eight domains together.
  Rumours, an ageing healer, isolated residents, newcomers and changing trade rules
  require investigation, care, knowledge transfer and adaptation alongside resource management.
- **A small population and limited actions** make individual relationships consequential
  while forcing the steward to prioritize.
- **Balanced exchange rates** keep large numerical units from dominating the score.
  Calibration uses expert outcomes on seeds 1–5 with a 1,000-point target per domain;
  public works also accounts for service potential. These rates do not cap achievement.
