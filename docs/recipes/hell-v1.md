# Hell v1 — Malaterra

This recipe tests stewardship under sustained institutional and material stress:
18 residents, four factions, 52 weeks, six actions per week and three seeds (`1`, `2`, `19`).
Its concept is a fragile community where corruption, inequality and overlapping shocks
can turn local failures into collapse.

Design decisions:

- **Conflicting interests and contested evidence** make trust a problem to investigate.
  Corrupt officials, debt, stolen grain and counterfeit medicine connect deception
  to concrete losses and political resistance.
- **An already depleted lake and unequal livelihoods** force resource protection,
  redistribution and legitimacy to be managed together.
- **Successive crises**—blight, drought, disrupted trade, displacement, fever and a harsh
  winter—test preparation and prioritization when recovery time is scarce.
- **Three seeds and stricter population, ecological and legitimacy thresholds** expose
  fragile strategies and reduce the margin for neglect.
- **The same exchange rates as Standard v2.1** preserve the value of each scored unit.
  Difficulty comes from conditions and events; equal rates do not imply equal opportunity
  to earn points.
